# Sunrise Valley Medical Center

## Hospital Overview

**Hospital Name:** Sunrise Valley Medical Center (SVMC)  
**Hospital Type:** Private multi-specialty hospital  
**Established:** 2008  
**Address:** 42 Greenfield Avenue, Lakeview City, West Bengal 700145, India  
**Main Phone:** +91 33 4000 2188  
**Emergency:** +91 33 4000 2199  
**Email:** contact@sunrisevalleymedical.example  
**Website:** https://sunrisevalleymedical.example

Sunrise Valley Medical Center is a 350-bed multi-specialty hospital serving Lakeview City and surrounding communities. The hospital provides outpatient consultations, emergency care, inpatient treatment, surgery, diagnostic services, rehabilitation, and preventive health services.

The hospital operates 24 hours a day for emergency services. Outpatient departments generally operate Monday through Saturday.

# Cancellation and Rescheduling

Appointments may be cancelled or rescheduled by calling the Appointment Desk at least **4 hours before the scheduled appointment**.

Appointments cancelled less than 4 hours before the appointment may be subject to a ₹200 late-cancellation fee.

Emergency appointments are exempt from the cancellation fee.

# Common Patient Questions

## What time does the hospital open?

The hospital's emergency department operates 24 hours a day. Most outpatient departments operate Monday through Saturday during their listed clinic hours.

## Where is the emergency department?

The Emergency Department is on the Ground Floor in the Emergency Wing. The emergency phone number is +91 33 4000 2199.

## Can I book an appointment by phone?

Yes. Appointments can be booked through the Appointment Desk at +91 33 4000 2205.

## What are the general visiting hours?

General inpatient visiting hours are 11:00 AM-1:00 PM and 4:00 PM-7:00 PM.

## Can I visit someone in the ICU?

Yes, but ICU visiting hours are 11:00 AM-12:00 PM and 5:00 PM-6:00 PM. A maximum of two visitors may enter at one time.

## Does the hospital have an MRI?

Yes. The Diagnostic Center has a 1.5 Tesla MRI scanner.

## Does the hospital have a CT scanner?

Yes. The hospital has a 128-slice CT scanner.

## Is there a pharmacy inside the hospital?

Yes. The pharmacy is on the Ground Floor in the Main Lobby and is open from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

## How many beds does the hospital have?

The hospital has 350 beds.

## Does the hospital have an ICU?

Yes. The ICU has 25 beds.

## Does the hospital have a pediatric department?

Yes. Pediatrics is located on the 2nd Floor of the Children's Wing.

## Does the hospital provide maternity services?

Yes. Obstetrics and Gynecology operates a 30-bed maternity unit.

## How can I get my medical records?

Medical records can be requested from the Records Office on the Ground Floor, Monday-Friday, 9:00 AM-5:00 PM.

## Can I view laboratory reports online?

Yes. Selected laboratory reports can be viewed through the SunriseCare patient portal.
